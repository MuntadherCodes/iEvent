Privacy Policy

iEvent is an event discovery and ticketing platform for Iraq, operated by Hillool OÜ (Estonia).

The short version: iEvent lets you discover events and book tickets, and lets organizers publish events and sell tickets. We only collect what we need to run the platform.

  • We never store your card details
  • We never sell your data
  • We never use your data for ads

01Who we are

iEvent ("iEvent", "we", "us") is a platform for discovering events and booking tickets in Iraq.

This policy explains how we handle your personal data.

02Information we collect

Account
Name, email, phone (optional), and password.
Tickets & bookings
Tickets you buy, QR codes, order history.
Payment data
Payments are processed by our payment providers.
Organizer data
Business details and payout details.
Device & usage
Device type, browser, language, approximate city.

03Payments & tickets

iEvent is a platform connecting attendees with organizers.

Refunds and cancellations are handled by the organizer.

04How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Show events, issue tickets and QR codes.
  • Let organizers publish events and receive earnings.
  • Send confirmations and notifications.
  • Provide support and prevent fraud.
  • Improve the Platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we never sell it.

05Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract
  • Legitimate interests
  • Consent
  • Legal obligation

Local Iraqi rules may also apply.

06Who we share your data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run iEvent:

Payment providers
Payment processing
Hosting
Website and database hosting
Analytics
Aggregated usage analytics
Organizers
Attendance details needed to fulfil your booking.

This list is not necessarily exhaustive.

07Organizers & attendees

iEvent is a two-sided platform.

iEvent does not control how organizers use that data outside the Platform.

08International data transfers

Your data may be transferred between Iraq, the EU, and our service providers.

Subject to review by local legal counsel.

09How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active.

10Your rights

You may have the right to access, correct, delete, and export your data.

EU users may also lodge a complaint with the competent authority.

11Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data.

12Age

iEvent is intended for people aged 18 or older.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time.
