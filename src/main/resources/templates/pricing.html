Pricing

One small fee, only on paid tickets

Hosting on iEvent has no subscription and no setup cost. Free events are always free.

Free tickets: 0 IQD, always

The booking fee, per paid ticket

It depends only on the ticket price. This is the whole formula:

Ticket price Booking fee
Up to 15,000 IQD 750 IQD flat
Above 15,000 IQD 3% + 2,000 IQD, capped at 15,000 IQD
Free tickets Always free. No fee, ever.

Examples: a 10,000 IQD ticket carries 750 IQD, a 25,000 IQD ticket carries 2,750 IQD, and a 500,000 IQD ticket carries 15,000 IQD, the cap.
